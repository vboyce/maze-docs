# Papers using A-maze

These are papers we know of that build on A-maze ([Boyce, Futrell, & Levy, 2020](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0749596X19301147); [Boyce & Levy, 2023](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/6vh9d8zm)). The list was assembled by going through the papers citing those two, and checking each one for actual use. It is surely incomplete. If you know of other work, including pre-prints, posters or theses, please send it and I'll add it.

## New experiments using A-maze

Papers that ran their own Maze experiments with A-maze distractors and/or the Ibex Maze controller, or that build a related method on A-maze.

- Abels & Neeleman (2026). Linear asymmetries and incremental parsing. *Language*.
- Bouso, Hundt, & Van Driessche (2024). A sisterhood of constructions? ... Objoid constructions. *Cognitive Linguistics*.
- Chacón, Kort, O'Neill, & Sorensen (2021). Limits on semantic prediction in the processing of extraction from adjunct clauses. OSF preprint.
- Duff, Brasoveanu, & Rysling (2021). Task influences on lexical underspecification: Insights from the Maze and SPR. CUNY.
- Han, Block, Gendron, Grant, et al. (2025). Underspecified *they* becomes specified early in sentence processing. *Language, Cognition and Neuroscience*.
- Han, Block, Gendron, et al. (2026). Resumptive pronouns form on-line dependencies with fillers in English: Evidence from the Maze task. *Glossa Psycholinguistics*.
- Hao, Fuchs, & Vasishth (2025). Similarity-based interference in the processing of classifier-noun dependencies in Mandarin Chinese. *Journal of Memory and Language*.
- Heuser & Gibson (2023). Transformer-Maze: An accessible incremental processing measurement tool. CogSci proceedings. (See also Heuser, 2022, MIT thesis.)
- Hoeks, Toosarvandani, & Rysling (2025). Discourse representations guide alternative set activation.
- Hundt, Dallas, & Van Driessche (2026). Preterite and present perfect… *Constructions*.
- Husband (2022). *Glossa Psycholinguistics*.
- Kim, Orth, & Yoshida (2024). Incremental structure building in the processing of ellipsis. *Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology*.
- Levinson (2023). Beyond surprising: English event structure in the Maze. *Experiments in Linguistic Meaning*.
- Maina-Kilaas & Levy (2026). Revisiting real-time digging-in effects: No evidence from NP/Z garden-paths. arXiv:2603.23624.
- Orth (2023). Active assignment of quantifier scope guides language processing. Dissertation.
- Orth, Nemeskey, & Ronai (2025/2026). *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition*.
- Orth & Yoshida (2022). Processing profile for quantifiers in verb phrase ellipsis: Evidence for grammatical economy. *Proceedings of the LSA*.
- Pushpita & Levy (2024). Image-conditioned human language comprehension and psychometric benchmarking of visual language models. CoNLL.

- (2026). Incremental processing of Tense Harmony and the disambiguation of clefted relatives. *Language, Cognition and Neuroscience*.

- Kajikawa, Isono, & Wilcox (2026). Information-theoretic storage cost in sentence comprehension. CoNLL.

- Shain (2024). Word frequency and predictability dissociate in naturalistic reading. *Open Mind*.
